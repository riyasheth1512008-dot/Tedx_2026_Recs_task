About TEDxNITKSurathkal

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience. At TEDxNITKSurathkal, live speakers and sparks of conversation combine to deep-dive into ideas across technology, entertainment, design, and society.
